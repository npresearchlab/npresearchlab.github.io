How the brain adapts, and how rehabilitation can help it

The Neural Plasticity Research Lab at Emory University studies the adaptive capacity of the human brain, to design new rehabilitation strategies for people living with stroke and other neurologic injury and disease.

Take part in a study See our research

About the lab

The Neural Plasticity Research Lab (NPRL) is a highly interdisciplinary and collaborative lab at Emory University. The mission of the NPRL is to improve our understanding of the adaptive capacity of the human brain in an effort to design novel rehabilitation strategies to mitigate the impact of neurologic injury and disease.

Our team brings together physical therapists, neuroscientists, biomedical engineers, and students from Emory and Georgia Tech. We work to create trusting spaces that are welcoming to everyone, from the community members who volunteer for our studies to the trainees we seek to recruit and retain.

Option A: current (live)

What we study

We use neuroimaging, brain stimulation, robotics, and virtual reality to see how the nervous system changes with learning, aging, and injury.

Balance and falls

How the brain senses body motion and keeps us upright, and why that changes with age and after a stroke.

Sensation and movement after stroke

Measuring how the brain uses touch, body position, and vision to guide the arm, and predicting who will recover.

Aging and navigation

How the brain's navigation network changes as we get older, studied with virtual reality and combined TMS and fMRI.
